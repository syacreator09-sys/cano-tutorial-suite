# Privacy

The suite has no analytics, advertising identifiers, background telemetry or maintainer-operated cloud service. Mock planning and job-state generation are local.

Live execution delegates data to separately configured skills and providers. Browser captures, authorized sessions, avatar scripts, provider identifiers, source media, generated assets, traces, captions and renders can contain private or confidential information. Keep them under `.runtime/` or other ignored local storage.

The maintainers do not receive local jobs or provider requests. Each enabled website, AI provider, storage service and renderer may process data under its own privacy notice and account settings.

Review and delete local job data when no longer needed. Publication must remain a separate human approval step.

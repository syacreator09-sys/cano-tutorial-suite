# Responsible Use Policy

Use the suite only for lawful, authorized and transparent tutorial production.

## Allowed

- Product tutorials, training, documentation, education and lawful marketing.
- Authorized browser demonstrations, Digital Twins, VideoVox scenes and licensed media.
- Mock planning and review before any paid or live operation.

## Not allowed

- Unauthorized account access, credential harvesting, bypassing safeguards or deceptive automation.
- Impersonation, undisclosed synthetic endorsements, fabricated evidence or misleading demonstrations.
- Publishing private, regulated, copyrighted or confidential material without authorization.
- Enabling provider spending, identity use or publication without the required human approval.

## Required approvals

The suite keeps these gates disabled by default:

- live browser access;
- provider spending;
- identity/avatar use;
- publication.

Operators must confirm authorization, privacy, rights, factual accuracy and provider terms before enabling each gate.
